Privacy policy

Netwerk collects nothing.

This is the whole policy, and it is short because there is little to report. Netwerk is an app without a server: there is no account, no backend, and not a single piece of data goes to the developer or to any third party.

What the app uses

Contacts

Netwerk reads your address book to show the names you add meaning to. That data is read on your device and sent nowhere. If you create a new contact inside the app, it is written to Apple Contacts — beyond that, Netwerk changes nothing about your contacts.

Calendar

Only when you tap “Find an event in your calendar” does Netwerk look at events from the week before and the week after, so you can turn one into a logged moment. Nothing is written to or changed in your calendar, and nothing from it is stored except the text you choose to keep.

Reminders

Only when you set a reminder yourself does Netwerk create one in Apple’s Reminders app.

What you write down

Your notes, topics, moments and connections live in a database on your device. If iCloud is switched on, that database syncs through your private iCloud storage to your other devices. That storage is yours; I have no access to it and cannot look inside.

Artificial intelligence

The summaries, topic suggestions and recognised names come from the language model Apple builds into the device itself (Apple Intelligence). No text goes to a cloud service — not to OpenAI, not to Google, not to me.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
  • No ads, no tracking, no advertising identifier.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No account, no login, no email address required.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and the local database is gone. To remove the synced copy as well, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage, where you can erase Netwerk’s data. Your contacts in Apple Contacts stay put; they were always yours.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects data from nobody, children included.

Changes

If anything about this policy changes, it will appear on this page, with a date.
